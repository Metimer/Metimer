## What I build

I work at the intersection of **data engineering**, **applied machine learning**, and **LLM applications**—with a focus on systems that remain useful after the demo.

- **LLM products:** RAG systems, tool-using agents, context design, and evaluation loops.
- **Data foundations:** ingestion, transformations, orchestration, and analytics-ready datasets.
- **Decision systems:** models and BI layers that make complex data actionable.

## How I work

From the first notebook to production, I optimise for the things that make AI products durable:

| Design for | In practice |
|---|---|
| **Quality** | clear evaluation criteria, retrieval testing, and feedback loops |
| **Reliability** | observable workflows, guardrails, and graceful failure modes |
| **Value** | useful metrics, cost awareness, and product-oriented iteration |
